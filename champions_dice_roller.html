Dice Roller & Damage Calculator

3d6 skill and attack rolls with exact odds, normal and killing damage worked through defenses, STUN, BODY and Knockback, and the Speed Chart.

Set a target, then roll.

    Work out the target

    Characteristic and characteristic-based skill rolls: 9 + CHAR/5 (13 gives 12-, 18 gives 13-). Attack roll: 11 + OCV − DCV, plus any modifiers. A 3 always succeeds and an 18 always fails.

    Exact Odds on 3d6

    There are 216 ways three dice can land, so every chance below is exact. The middle of the curve is steep: one point either side of 10- or 11- moves your chance by about twelve percentage points.

    Damage

      Normal damage: STUN is the total of the dice; each die counts 0 BODY on a 1, 1 on 2–5 and 2 on a 6 (a ½d6 counts half its roll, rounded up, in STUN, and 1 BODY on 4–6). Killing damage: BODY is the total; STUN is BODY × ½d6 (1 to 3). Defenses come off both; only Resistant defense stops killing BODY. More STUN taken than CON from one attack and the target is Stunned. Knockback: roll 2d6 (3d6 for a killing attack) and subtract it from the BODY rolled; 0 is knocked down, each point above is 2 metres.
